About BATIMIX

After the same sleepless night, some people deteriorate a lot and others barely at all. Cui et al. (2026) proposed that the increase in the variability of theta power in resting EEG predicts that deterioration (ρ ≈ 0.55 with reaction time, n = 28). BATIMIX tests that idea, person by person, on public data. It is an exploratory screening tool for sleep researchers, not a diagnosis.

Preliminary conclusion

Without sleep, each person's theta changes in different directions, and that change does not robustly predict who deteriorates: across 896 alternative analyses (two preprocessing pipelines) no joint statistic exceeds chance. The only consistent hint is with mood (positive affect), not with vigilance.

Data

OpenNeuro ds004902 (Xiang, Fan, Bai, Lv and Lei, 2024, Scientific Data, CC0): 71 people recorded after normal sleep (NS) and after 24–30 hours without sleep (SD), in counterbalanced order. Eyes-open resting EEG, 61 electrodes, 500 Hz.

Glossary

Theta (θ)Brain electrical activity at 4–8 Hz; it rises with sleep pressure.
μθ, σθRelative theta power (4–8 Hz divided by 1–40 Hz, unitless): its mean (μθ) and its variability across 75 4-s epochs (σθ).
ΔChange SD minus NS. Positive = increases after deprivation.
PVTPsychomotor Vigilance Task: median reaction time (RT, ms), lapses and RT SD.
KSS, SSSKarolinska and Stanford sleepiness scales (points).
PANAS+, PANAS−Positive and negative affect (points).
Vulnerable quadrantAbove the median theta change and worse than the median of the chosen marker.
t, dz, 95% CIPaired t-test per electrode with all 68 people: t with 67 degrees of freedom; dz = mean Δ divided by its standard deviation (paired effect size; 0.2 small, 0.5 medium); 95% CI of the mean Δ. Shown when hovering over an electrode.
FDRBenjamini–Hochberg correction for 61 paired t-tests, one per electrode.
C(e)Consistency of a group at electrode e: share of people whose Δ rises minus share whose Δ falls (from −1 to +1). Close to ±1 = the group changes in the same direction.
Vulnerable, resilientHeatmap groups, defined by behavior only: worse or not than the median change in the chosen marker. They do not use theta, so that each group's theta signature is not circular.

Method

Preprocessing (tools/eeg_ica.py): 50 Hz notch, 1–40 Hz band-pass, bad channels interpolated, FCz re-inserted and average reference, extended Infomax ICA (30 components) removing ocular components (correlation with Fp1/Fp2 an outlier, or ICLabel eye) and muscle components (ICLabel p > 0.85), 75 4-s epochs with those above 200 µV rejected (71 of 75 kept on average). This follows the sleep-deprivation EEG literature (Snipes et al., 2022; Lian et al., 2023; Ma et al., 2023; Cui et al., 2026); the Week 6 pipeline (FCz reference, no ICA) is kept as a robustness dimension. Maps by biharmonic v4 interpolation, as in EEGLAB and FieldTrip, on the real electrode positions (electrodes.tsv of ds004902) in azimuthal equidistant projection; the panel selector also shows MNE's cubic interpolation and the spherical spline (Perrin et al., 1989; m = 4, 20 Legendre terms, no regularization). All three implementations reproduce the originals (EEGLAB and FieldTrip in MATLAB, MNE 1.8) with an error below 1e-9 of the range. NS and SD share a sequential scale per row; Δ uses a diverging scale with a neutral zero. With a selected group the maps average those people, without a per-electrode test. Spearman correlations with 95% CI.

Map interpolation

The «Interp.» selector of the topomap panel only changes how the space between electrodes is painted. The values at each electrode, the t-tests, the FDR q values, the rings and the fills are the same with any method. v4 (default) is the biharmonic interpolation of EEGLAB topoplot and FieldTrip ft_topoplotER; «cubic · MNE» is that of MNE-Python plot_topomap; «spherical spline» interpolates on the 3D positions (Perrin et al., 1989). If a pattern appears with one method and not with the others, it is an effect of the drawing, not of the data.

Robustness: multiverse of 896 analyses

To respond honestly to the non-replication of Cui et al. (2026), 896 specifications were computed: 2 preprocessing pipelines (average reference with ICA, or FCz reference without ICA) × 4 regions × 2 metrics (σθ, μθ) × 2 forms of change (absolute, relative) × 7 behavioral markers × 2 correlations (Spearman, Pearson) × 2 outlier rules. Joint inference permutes the behavioral rows among the 68 people 2000 times (Simonsohn, Simmons and Nelson, 2020). No joint statistic exceeds chance:

Secondary views

The right column collapses with ›. Its first two views are robustness checks that defend the main finding; the third is exploratory. «Oscillatory or 1/f?» separates the rhythmic part from the 1/f background of the spectrum with specparam (settings of Bai et al., 2024, on this same dataset) and compares each person's change in σθ with that of their periodic theta: they match (ρ = 0.82 frontal, 0.89 centrotemporal), so theta variability is not an effect of the 1/f background. «θ synchrony between regions» uses wPLI² with average reference between 10 regions: it decreases in 44 of 45 pairs, but none survives FDR; it is exploratory. «Driven by blinks?» checks what ICA leaves: it detects blinks on Fp1/Fp2 of the raw EEG and recomputes σθ without the windows that touch a blink. Each person's Δσθ barely changes (ρ = 0.95 frontal, 0.96 centrotemporal), so blinks do not drive it; people whose blink rate rose more do show a larger Δσθ (ρ = 0.34), which is more likely a shared cause (drowsiness) than contamination. All views follow your selection (without a statistical test in subgroups) and are generated from the raw EEG with tools/build_secondary.py and tools/build_quality.py. Hover any person in the scatter or heatmap to see their EEG quality.

Limitations

PVT paired for only 30 people (low power); automatic artifact selection (no visual review of components). Rerunning the raw EEG with the exact pipeline of Cui et al. (1–30 Hz, 4–30 Hz denominator) raises the frontal group effect (t = 2.32, p = 0.023), but the link with ΔPVT stays null (ρ = −0.06, n = 30; no subset of 28 exceeds 0.14), so the pipeline does not explain the non-replication.
