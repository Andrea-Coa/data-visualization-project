1. Feedback received

Instructor's remarks in the design review. There should be no white space between charts (no wasted space); the flow should be smooth; the interface should be friendly and free of visual noise: no overlapping text, and filters and interactive tools should not add noise.

Team decisions after the review. No scrollytelling or animations (motion graphics): the whole analysis must fit in a single view. One header with all the controls; remove the texts that explain each chart and how to use it; remove the space between visualizations; replace the toggle-based topomap with a 2 × 3 matrix (NS, SD, Δ by σθ and μθ, or by two people chosen in the scatter). After reviewing a first redesign, the team also asked for a complete visual change and for removing the noise in the multiverse panel.

Questions we asked the reviewers (deliveries/week10/PrototypeReport.md): six questions about the introduction, the lasso, map aggregation, the compact layout, how missing data are communicated, and the interpolated field. We answer them below with what changed.

Later literature review (research/literatura_verificada.md and research/informe_conectividad.md, in Spanish): methodological suggestions that arose while verifying the state of the art.
